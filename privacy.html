Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page is a starter privacy notice for the Mac&Talk website. The site may use Supabase to manage accounts, authentication, profile details, live-session assignments, and podcast metadata. Audio files may be stored in private storage and played using time-limited links.

Information handled

  • Account information such as email address, username, profile name, and role.
  • Session and podcast information submitted by members, including titles, descriptions, schedules, and uploaded audio.
  • Technical information needed for sign-in, security, and reliable service operation.

How information is used

Information is used to operate member accounts, display approved sessions and published podcasts, manage invitations, and protect the platform. Administrators may access account and moderation information needed to operate the service.

Audio and microphone permissions

Creator Studio may request microphone access when you choose to join or start a live session or make a recording. Your browser controls this permission. Do not record or publish another person's voice without appropriate consent.

Storage and retention

Data is processed by the configured hosting and database providers. Account and media retention depends on the platform configuration and administrator actions. Contact the Mac&Talk administrator for access, correction, or deletion requests.

Important

This is a practical starter notice, not legal advice or a verified statement of every provider setting. Before public launch, replace it with a policy that matches your actual data practices, applicable law, retention periods, and contact details.
